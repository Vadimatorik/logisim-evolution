74156

The 74156 is a digital model of a dual 2-line to 4-line decoder/demultiplexer with open-collector outputs. It follows the HD74HC156 / 74HC156 function table. It does not model output current or the voltage an external pull-up may rise to.

A and B are shared. A is the least significant bit. Section 1 (1Y0 to 1Y3) pulls one output low only when 1G is low and 1C is high. Section 2 (2Y0 to 2Y3) pulls one output low only when 2G is low and 2C is low. Every output that is not selected is released. In the simulator a released output is an unknown value. Place a pull resistor on each output to read it as high, and to wire-AND several open-collector outputs together.

Used as a demultiplexer, the selected output of section 1 is the inverse of 1C, and the selected output of section 2 follows 2C. Tie 1C to 2C and 1G to 2G to decode three bits into eight open-collector outputs.

The HDL model emits the same function as strong low and high levels, the result seen with pull-ups. It does not emit high-impedance.
