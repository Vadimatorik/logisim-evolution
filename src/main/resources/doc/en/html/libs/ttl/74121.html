74121

The 74121 is a digital model of a non-retriggerable monostable. It follows the SN74121 function table and pinout. It does not simulate the analog capacitor. The external timing pins are attributes, not wires. There is no HDL model.

For a capacitor above 1000 pF the pulse width is tW = 0.7 × R(kΩ) × C(pF) nanoseconds. That width is converted to simulator ticks at the circuit tick frequency and is remembered when the pulse starts. A later change of R or C applies on the next trigger. If the tick frequency is low, a short pulse lasts one tick: at 1 Hz that is one second. Raise the tick frequency to observe a shorter pulse.

The device is not retriggerable and has no reset. A new edge during the pulse does not extend it, and the inputs cannot cut it short. Schmitt thresholds on B, the correction for a small capacitor, and the pulse with no external capacitor are not modeled.

Rext is an integer number of kiloohms from 2 to 40. The data sheet starts at 1.4 kΩ, which an integer attribute cannot store. Cext is in picofarads, from 1000 pF upward. Choosing the internal resistor uses the typical 2 kΩ instead of Rext.
